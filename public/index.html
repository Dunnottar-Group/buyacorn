The Founding Member offer

Acorn is $5,000/mo at retail. Founding Members lock in $2,500/mo, half of retail, for life, as long as the membership stays in good standing. One price per company, not per seat. Twenty-five seats means twenty-five founding companies.

Each member pays their own EC2, Slack workspace, and model API usage directly. The subscription covers Acorn only.

Annual option: $25,000/yr for Founding Members or $50,000/yr at retail. Ten months billed, two months free: Founding Members save $5,000/yr, Standard Members save $10,000/yr.

Refund guarantee

A $2,500 deposit secures a Founding seat and covers the first month. Refundable up until the second subscription payment.

Founding Members can refund their most recent monthly payment at any point in the membership, for life. Never more than one month of exposure.

Standard Members can cancel anytime with no future charges. Refund available during the first month only.

Annual plans refund in full during the first 30 days, prorated after that.

This guarantee covers Acorn's own fees. Your server and API costs are billed to you directly and are not part of it.

Referral

Members earn 20% of a referred member's payments, for the life of the referred member's subscription. Earnings apply as credit against the referrer's own subscription first. Credit beyond the subscription amount pays out as cash, uncapped. A refund reduces credit balance only. Cash already paid is never clawed back.

Referral eligibility requires the referrer to be an Acorn member.

This Interim Agreement (the "Agreement") is between [ENTITY] ("Provider," "we," "us") and the individual or company accepting it ("Member," "you"), effective on acceptance ([EFFECTIVE-DATE]).

1. What Acorn is

Acorn is pre-release software: a sovereign AI chief of staff that runs on infrastructure you own and control, your cloud server, your team chat workspace, your AI-provider accounts. You are joining the Alpha Program to help test and improve it.

2. The alpha grant: Acorn, free for life

As thanks for your testing participation, Provider grants you a perpetual, royalty-free, non-transferable license to run one instance of Acorn for your own business use, at no Acorn subscription fee, for as long as you wish. This grant is personal to you (or your company) and may not be resold, sublicensed, or assigned without our written consent. It covers the Acorn software and updates. It does not cover the third-party infrastructure and services you run it on (Section 4).

3. Alpha status: provided "as is"

Acorn is pre-release, evolving software provided "as is" and "as available." To the fullest extent permitted by law, Provider disclaims all warranties, express or implied, including merchantability, fitness for a particular purpose, and non-infringement. Alpha software may contain defects, may change or break between updates, and should not be relied upon as the sole system of record for any critical business decision. You use it at your own risk and are responsible for your own backups and verification of anything that matters.

4. Sovereignty: your infrastructure, your keys, your data

This is the core of Acorn and a non-negotiable design line.

  • You provision and pay for your own infrastructure: your cloud compute (for example an AWS EC2 instance), your team-chat workspace, and your own AI-provider API keys (Anthropic, xAI/Grok, Google, and similar). These are billed to you directly by those providers. Onboarding walks you through this.
  • Provider never holds, stores, or has standing access to your provider API keys or your business data. Your keys live in your own secrets store (for example AWS Secrets Manager) in your own account. We cannot read them and do not want to.
  • You own your data. Everything Acorn captures, writes, or reasons over on your instance is yours.
  • Provisioning may report step-completion status back to us (for example, secrets configured: yes or no) so we can help you finish setup. This is a completion signal only and never includes the underlying secret values or your business data (Section 6).

5. Updates and the self-improving loop: your consents

By accepting, and again confirmed during onboarding, you consent to:

  • Automatic updates (AUTO_UPDATE, on by default, disclosed): your instance will fetch and apply improvements. You may opt out.
  • The self-improving loop (data-sharing tier 3, on by default): your instance shares distilled learnings that help every Acorn improve. You may reduce this to health and telemetry only (tier 2 or below) at any time with a single command. What crosses the wire is defined by a strict schema. Your business content is end-to-end encrypted to us and never transits in readable form, and health signals carry no business content. See the data-share tiers disclosed at onboarding.

You will give these consents on the record during onboarding. Nothing here waives that step.

6. Status reporting without data access

So we can support your onboarding and operation, your instance may transmit completion and health signals only, for example which setup steps are done, software version, whether services are healthy, error counts. These signals are structured booleans and counters. They never include your API keys, secrets, message content, files, or any business data, and Provider has no mechanism to retrieve those through this channel.

7. Feedback

The alpha is about learning from real use. Sharing feedback, and joining periodic check-in calls, is encouraged and invited but not required; there is no obligation to participate on any schedule. If you do give feedback, you grant Provider a perpetual, royalty-free license to use it to improve Acorn, with no obligation to compensate you. Being named as a reference or providing a testimonial is entirely optional and only with your consent.

8. Confidentiality

As a pre-release participant you may see unreleased features, roadmaps, and pricing. Please keep non-public information about Acorn's internals confidential during the alpha. This does not restrict your right to publicly describe your own experience using Acorn.

9. Intellectual property

Acorn, its software, and all improvements remain Provider's property. This Agreement grants a license to use, not any ownership of, the software. Your data and your own materials remain yours.

10. No paid obligation; conversion is optional and manual

You will not be charged as an alpha member, and the alpha does not automatically convert to any paid plan. If you and Provider later choose a paid Founding Member relationship, that is a separate, mutually agreed, manually initiated step, never automatic.

11. Limitation of liability

To the fullest extent permitted by law, Provider's total liability arising out of or relating to this Agreement or Acorn will not exceed USD $100, and Provider will not be liable for indirect, incidental, consequential, special, or punitive damages, or for lost profits or data. Because the alpha is provided free of charge, you acknowledge this allocation of risk is reasonable.

12. Term and termination

Either party may end participation at any time on written notice. Your perpetual license under Section 2 survives termination of the program unless you breach this Agreement (for example, resale, or misuse of confidential information), in which case the license may be revoked. Sections 3, 7, 8, 9, and 11 survive.
